Mochi Noki Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how the operator of Mochi Noki ("we") handles information in the Mochi Noki app for macOS and on this website. It follows the structure required by Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (Article 30).

This English version is provided for reference. The Korean version is authoritative; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Why information is processed

Information is processed only for these purposes; if they change, we'll update this policy first.

  • Raising your pet and keeping your daily records (water, stretching, focus, habits, plans)
  • If you turn them on: showing and reminding you of Mac Calendar events, and dressing your pet for the weather outside
  • Checking whether you have Noki Plus
  • Answering you when you contact us (for example by email)

2. What is processed

① Processed on your Mac (on-device)

Mochi Noki's features process information on your device. It isn't sent to any server, and it's deleted from your Mac when you turn the feature off or remove the app (how to remove the app's data folder: section 5).

  • Your pet: name, stage and look, stats, outfits, album, diary, collection, achievements and quest progress
  • What you enter: habits and their history, plans (title, date, time, note), water logs and cups, mini game records
  • Settings: reminders, appearance, language, notch side and so on
  • Mac status (not stored; only feeds the pet's daily counts): battery level and charging, thermal state, CPU load, time since the last keyboard or mouse input (never what you type), whether the screen is asleep, and whether the front window fills the screen (app names, window titles and contents are never read)
  • Mac Calendar (only if you connect it and allow access in macOS): the next seven days of events in the calendars you pick, read to show and remind you, never stored. If you turn on "Add my plans to Calendar", plans made in the app are written to the calendar you choose, and the app only changes or deletes the events it added.
  • Backups: "Export backup" writes the above to a file where you choose. On updates the app keeps copies of the previous save (the last three), a copy made just before you import a backup, and a copy of any save a new version couldn't read (kept aside rather than deleted), all in the same folder.

The app uses no camera, microphone, location, contacts, Accessibility, Input Monitoring or Screen Recording access.

② Sent out only when you turn it on

  • Weather (off by default): the city name you type, its coordinates (two decimals, about 1 km) and the app's language code are sent to the weather service Open-Meteo. Turning weather off also deletes the saved city and reading. Details: section 9.

③ Payments

Noki Plus purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive payment or Apple Account details; the app only checks the signed purchase records Apple keeps on your Mac to see whether Plus is on. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

④ What you send yourself

  • Battle codes: "Copy my code" puts your pet's name, stage and look, level, stats, tricks, outfits, pattern and random pet and collection identifiers on your clipboard. It reaches only whoever you paste it to, never us.
  • Feedback and questions: "Send feedback" only copies the app and macOS versions, Mac model, display setup, language and a short pet summary to your clipboard; you decide whether to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

3. Children under 14

There is no sign-up, and we collect no one's personal information, children included. If a child under 14 emails us, we use the message only as needed to answer and delete it once we learn of it, unless a parent or guardian consents.

4. How long it's kept

  • On your Mac: until you delete it. We keep nothing.
  • Copies of the previous save made on updates: the last three; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Weather requests: we have no server and keep nothing. Open-Meteo says it deletes its web server logs after 90 days (section 9).
  • Emails: kept for one year after we answer, then deleted, or right away if you ask.

5. How it's deleted

  • App data: move the app to the Trash and, in Finder, delete the "Mochi Noki" folder under ~/Library/Containers/. That removes your pet and records.
  • Per feature: turning weather off deletes the saved city and reading; disconnecting Calendar clears the events read; events the app added to Calendar can be deleted in the Calendar app.
  • Exported backups: delete them where you saved them.
  • Emails: deleted beyond recovery when the retention period ends.

6. Sharing with third parties

We don't share personal information with third parties. The weather feature is the app asking a weather service directly, for a feature you turned on; see section 9.

7. Further use

Not applicable. We don't use or share information beyond the purposes above.

8. Processors

Not applicable. We run no servers and use no processors.

9. Transfers abroad

Only if you turn weather on, the following is sent abroad. With weather off, nothing is.

RecipientOpenMeteo GmbH (Switzerland) · info@open-meteo.com
CountriesOpen-Meteo says its servers run in Europe and North America (countries not stated).
When and howAbout once an hour while weather is on, and when a city is first looked up; over HTTPS, without cookies
WhatCity name, coordinates (two decimals), the app's language code. As with any internet request, your IP address reaches the service.
WhyTo find the city's coordinates and read the current weather
RetentionPer Open-Meteo, web server logs are deleted after 90 days (Open-Meteo terms and privacy notice)
How to refuseTurn weather off in ⚙️ › Weather. Sending stops, and the pet no longer dresses for the weather; nothing else changes.

10. Security measures

  • Information is processed only on your Mac; we have no servers or account systems.
  • The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and reaches only what you allow.
  • Weather requests are sent only over encrypted HTTPS.

11. Sensitive information

Not applicable. We don't process sensitive information.

12. Pseudonymized information

Not applicable.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

The app and this website use no cookies, analytics or advertising identifiers. The website remembers only your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage, which is never sent to us. This website is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits, including IP addresses (GitHub Privacy Statement). Its fonts load from Google Fonts.

14. Behavioral information and ads

Not applicable. We collect no behavioral information for advertising, and the app has no ads.

15. Your rights

You (or a parent or guardian for children under 14) can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information at any time. Since it lives on your Mac, you can do most of this yourself in the app.

  • See: in the app, or all of it as a file with ⚙️ › Data › Export backup
  • Correct: edit your pet's name, habits, plans and water logs in the app
  • Delete: per record, or everything as in section 5
  • Stop: turn Calendar or weather off, or withdraw access in System Settings › Privacy & Security
  • For anything you emailed us, ask the privacy officer below and we'll act without delay.

16. Automated decisions

Not applicable. We make no fully automated decisions that significantly affect you. If such a feature is ever added, this policy will be updated first.
