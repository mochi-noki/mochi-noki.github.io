Mochi Noki · a Mac app

Knock knock, it's Noki!

A soft little friend who lives beside your MacBook's notch. While you work it reminds you to drink water, stretch and take real breaks, and to keep today's plans. Look after yourself, and it hatches from an egg and grows up with you.

💧 Water🙆 Stretching🎯 Focus timer🌱 Habit calendar📅 Plans🗓️ Mac Calendar🎈 13 expressions☂️ Weather🏆 35 achievements🎾 Play

Five friends, all free

☁️ Mongsil

a cloud baby

Its mood is the weather: drizzle when hungry, sunshine once today's water goal is met.

🧶 Boksle

a furball

Lies flat on its belly while charging; its ears perk up under the pointer.

🍮 Tangtang

a pudding

Melts, caramel dripping, when the Mac runs hot; wiggles happily while charging.

🌱 Kongkong

a sprout bean

Sparkles once today's water goal is met; its leaf droops when sleepy.

🌙 Mallo

a moon-bunny marshmallow

After 9 pm the crescent on its forehead glows.

Seasonal friends

Noki Plus members meet a limited friend each season, and keep it after the season and after Plus: Eunhaeng (a ginkgo mochi) and Danpung (a maple leaf) in autumn, Noonsongi (a fluffy snowball) and Hoppang (a steamed bun in a scarf) in winter.

It breathes with your Mac

Noki reacts to the battery, charging, heat, keyboard and mouse activity and the screen going to sleep, all read on your Mac. Its face shows thirteen feelings, and it can wear an umbrella or a scarf for the weather outside if you turn that on.

Looks after your day

💧 Water and stretching

Log a glass with one click, keep your own cups, add one you had away from the Mac. Gentle stretch reminders after long sitting, and 25- or 50-minute focus timers.

🌱 Habits and plans

Your own habits by count or time, a monthly habit calendar, simple plans with reminders, and (if you connect it) your Mac Calendar.

🏆 Quests and achievements

Three daily quests, 35 achievements and weekly and monthly challenges, rewarded with snacks to buy outfits.

🎾 Play

Pet it, throw it a ball on your screen, play three mini games, or battle a friend by swapping codes.

Light, and only on your Mac

Its breathing doesn't redraw anything, so it idles near 0% CPU and rests when the screen sleeps. Your pet and records stay on your Mac: no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

Coming soon

Mochi Noki is being polished with friends. We'll post here and in What's new when it's on the Mac App Store.

  • Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • On a Mac without a notch, or on an external display, a virtual notch appears at the top center. You can switch to a menu bar icon instead.
  • The five friends and the core features are free. Noki Plus (optional) adds unlimited habits, Noki's monthly letter and seasonal friends.
