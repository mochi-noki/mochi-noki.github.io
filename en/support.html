Support

Which Macs does it run on?
Apple silicon Macs (M1 or later) with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. On a Mac without a notch, or on an external display, a virtual notch appears at the top center; you can switch to a menu bar icon in ⚙️ › Pet & notch.
I can't see Noki
Try ⚙️ › "Find the notch again". Right after plugging in or removing a display it takes a moment to settle. Over full-screen apps it rests on purpose.
How do I back up my data or move it to another Mac?
⚙️ › Data › Export backup makes one file; open it on the other Mac with Import backup. Before importing, a copy of the current data is kept aside. Updates also keep the previous save automatically.
My Mac Calendar doesn't show up
In ⚙️ › Mac Calendar, choose "Connect Calendar" and allow access when macOS asks. If you declined before, turn Mochi Noki on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars. Pick which calendars to show on the same card.
What is Noki Plus, and how do I restore it?
The five friends and the core features are free. Noki Plus (optional) adds unlimited habits, Noki's monthly letter and report, and seasonal friends. If you bought it on another Mac or reinstalled, use Shop › Plus › "Restore purchases". Cancel or change a subscription in the App Store's subscription settings (the shop's "Manage subscription" button).
Do seasonal friends leave when the season ends?
No. A friend you've met stays after the season and after Plus. New eggs are only offered during their season.
There are too many reminders
Change the stretch and water intervals, or turn them off, in ⚙️ › Reminders. At night, during a focus timer and in full screen, reminders wait quietly.
Does it use much battery?
No. It idles near 0% CPU and rests when the screen sleeps.
What data does it collect?
No account, no server, and your records stay on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
